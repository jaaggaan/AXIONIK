Every Store Visit Is An
Untapped Opportunity.

"What if connecting to store Wi-Fi could become the beginning of a meaningful customer relationship?"

Customers walk into physical retail stores every day with genuine purchase intent. Yet the relationship between retailers and their in-store visitors often ends the moment a customer walks out. In a world of digital engagement, the physical store visit remains one of retail's most valuable — and underutilised — moments.

🏬
Physical Presence
Customers enter stores with active purchase intent, browsing, comparing, and experiencing products in person.
📶
Underused Wi-Fi
Guest Wi-Fi is widely deployed in retail stores but is typically treated as connectivity only — a missed engagement touchpoint.
🔗
Missing Bridge
There is rarely a structured mechanism connecting a customer's physical visit with ongoing digital engagement after they leave the store.

The Retail Engagement Challenge

  • Customers enter physical stores but leave without any digital connection being established
  • In-store Wi-Fi is provided as a convenience without capturing meaningful customer information
  • Retailers lack a standardised way to begin consent-based customer relationships during a store visit
  • Fashion and lifestyle brands invest heavily in in-store experience, but rarely extend that experience digitally from within the store environment
  • Repeat-visit engagement and personalised campaign delivery require customer data that in-store teams currently struggle to collect at scale
  • Every unacquired store visitor is a potential loyalty relationship that was never started

Portal Consent: The Foundation of Everything

Before any customer data is captured or used, AXIONIK requires explicit, informed consent through the captive portal. This is not automatic. Customers actively agree to receive communications, offers, and notifications from Shoppers Stop before engaging further with the platform. Consent-first architecture ensures every customer relationship begins ethically and in compliance with data privacy requirements.

Give Rewards. Create Experiences.
